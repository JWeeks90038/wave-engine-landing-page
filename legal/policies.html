1.6 Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the disclosed purpose, legal/accounting obligations, security, and dispute resolution, then delete, de-identify, or securely dispose of it where feasible. Retention depends on the type of information and the applicable purpose:

  • Apparel cart and checkout attempt: browser session storage; normally removed when the browser session ends or the user clears site data.
  • Paid apparel orders: Firestore records include customer, shipping, items, payment, and fulfillment details. The application does not set an automatic time-based deletion period.
  • Stripe transaction records: maintained by Stripe under its own retention and legal requirements.
  • Artist account, profile, uploads, review, and purchase records: retained as needed to provide the service, support transactions, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. The application does not set an automatic time-based deletion period for these records.
  • Album-art prompts and credit history: the application limits the recent image-history list to 100 entries and the credit transaction list to 250 entries; it does not set a time-based retention period for user/payment records. OpenAI retains information under its own policies.
  • Support email and technical logs: retained as needed to respond to requests, protect the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.
  • Privacy-request verification data: used only to verify and process a request, prevent fraud, and document compliance; delete when no longer needed, subject to legal obligations.

3. Refund, Return, Exchange, Cancellation, and Order Policy

This Policy applies to orders placed directly with Wave Engine Studio through https://waveenginemusic.com, including artist purchases and contributions made through connected Stripe accounts. Nothing in this Policy waives rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.

3.1 Physical apparel

Return window: Contact us within 30 calendar days after delivery to request a return for an eligible change-of-mind, fit, or size issue. The return must be approved before the item is shipped back.

Eligible condition and packaging: Unless damaged, defective, incorrect, or subject to a non-waivable legal right, returned apparel must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, free from odors, with original tags attached, and in resalable condition. Original packaging is preferred but is not required if the item is returned using reasonable protective packaging. Include proof of purchase or the order number. Do not return an item until we issue return instructions.

Damaged, defective, incorrect, or missing item: Email waveenginestudio@gmail.com with the order number, a description of the issue, and clear photographs of the item, packaging, shipping label, and issue where reasonably available. Contact us promptly and, where reasonably possible, within 30 calendar days after delivery or after discovering the issue. We may ask that the item or packaging be retained or returned for inspection. For a missing item, contact us promptly so we can investigate the order and carrier records.

If we confirm an error, damage, defect, or failure to deliver for which we are responsible, we will provide the remedy required by law and, where appropriate, a replacement, exchange, or refund. We will provide return instructions and pay reasonable return shipping for an item we sent incorrectly or that is confirmed defective.

Wrong size or change of mind: Returns or exchanges are available only within the 30-day return window and subject to the condition requirements above. The customer is responsible for return shipping for a non-defective change-of-mind, fit, or size return. Exchanges depend on available inventory and are not guaranteed. If the requested size is unavailable, we will provide a refund to the original payment method or store credit if the customer chooses store credit.

Refunds and store credit: Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method unless applicable law or the customer’s agreement permits another method. We will initiate an approved refund within 10 business days after receiving and reviewing the returned item. The payment processor or bank may require additional time to post the refund. Store credit is available only if the customer voluntarily chooses it and does not replace a cash refund where a cash refund is legally required.

Sale, clearance, promotional, and custom items: Wave Engine Studio currently does not designate apparel as final sale unless the product page or checkout clearly states that the item is final sale before purchase. Clearly labeled final-sale items are not eligible for discretionary change-of-mind returns, but remain eligible for remedies involving defects, incorrect fulfillment, non-delivery, or other rights that cannot legally be excluded. Custom or personalized items are not automatically exempt from remedies for defects or seller error.

Lost, stolen, refused, or undeliverable packages: Contact us with your order number. We will review the order, tracking information, delivery scan, and carrier investigation. If a carrier confirms that a package was lost or delivered incorrectly, we will work with the customer to provide an appropriate remedy, which may include reshipment or a refund. For packages marked delivered but reported stolen, we may require the customer to contact the carrier or file a carrier claim before determining whether replacement or refund is available. We are not responsible for theft after confirmed delivery to the address provided by the customer, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

If a package is returned as undeliverable or refused, we will contact the customer about reshipment or refund. Reshipment may require payment of actual additional shipping costs. Any deduction from a refund will be disclosed and limited to actual, lawful costs associated with the circumstances.

Customer address errors: Contact us promptly. We will try to correct an address before shipment but cannot guarantee a change after the order has been submitted or dispatched. If an order is returned or redirected because the customer supplied an incorrect or incomplete address, the customer may request reshipment at the customer’s expense. If the customer instead requests a refund after the item is returned, the product price will be refunded, but actual shipping or carrier costs may be deducted where lawful and applicable.

Cancellation before shipment: Email waveenginestudio@gmail.com with “Cancel order” and the order number. We will cancel and refund an order if the cancellation request is received before fulfillment begins or before the package is shipped. Once fulfillment has begun, a shipping label has been created, or the parcel has been dispatched, cancellation may not be possible and the applicable return rules will apply.

Out of stock or shipment delay: Inventory is checked at checkout but is not considered reserved until the order is processed. We will provide the shipping timeframe stated at checkout or in the product listing. If we cannot ship within the promised timeframe or within the period required by applicable law, we will provide legally required delay notice, obtain the customer’s consent to a delay where required, or cancel the order and promptly refund all amounts paid for unshipped merchandise. We will not treat silence as consent except where applicable law expressly permits it.
